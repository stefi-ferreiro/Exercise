Colors

A color palette is a carefully chosen set of colors that define a brand’s visual identity. It creates harmony, consistency, and emotional impact across designs. When used effectively, it guides attention, improves readability, and strengthens the overall user experience.

Typography

Inter is the primary typeface for the Teacher experience, chosen for its clarity, neutrality, and strong performance across information-dense interfaces.

Its clean forms and highly legible structure support the way teachers consume information: analysing student progress, interpreting metrics, reviewing rubrics, and making decisions based on data.

The type scale creates clear hierarchy and structure, helping users quickly distinguish between key information, supporting content, and actions.
